# denkr 0.10.0

## What changed

- Chat has compact message bubbles and a frosted input. Use the paperclip for attachments. The button on the other side switches between dictation, send, and stop.
- The sidebar slides over chat. The dim layer fades in place, and a slim accent marks the current chat. Tap your name or avatar to open Profile, or the gear to open Settings.
- Sheets, pickers, and message actions share the input’s blur. They blur the content behind them without blurring their own labels.
- Settings, onboarding, and the other screens share the same spacing, typography, and controls. The Profile form no longer has an outer border.

## Install

Download `denkr-0.10.0.apk` from this release and open it on your Android phone. If you already use the beta, install it over your current version. Do not uninstall first.
