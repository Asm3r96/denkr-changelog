# denkr 0.5.0

## Summary

This update makes it easier to manage your conversations, memories, notifications, and provider connections.

## What changed

- Explore saved memories in a new interactive Memory Galaxy.
- Edit or delete your messages, including continuing a conversation from an earlier message.
- Manage reminders, proactive messages, and notification preferences in one place.
- Connect an xAI account without entering an API key and access more Grok models.
- Search X posts and discussions through your assistant when an xAI account is connected.
- Enjoy smoother typing in long messages and improved chat layout.

## Notes

- X search and xAI account connection are experimental and may depend on xAI account access, limits, or service availability.
- Android beta installs are still manual outside Google Play.
