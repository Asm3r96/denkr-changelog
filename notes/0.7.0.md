# denkr 0.7.0

## Summary

This release replaces the experimental live voice call with faster in-chat dictation, adds Gemini 3.8 Flash options for connected Google accounts, and removes unfinished paths that were making the app harder to maintain.

## What changed

### In-chat dictation

- Tap the microphone in the composer to record a voice message, then tap again to transcribe it into the draft.
- Dictation uses a connected Gemini Google account and keeps the recorded audio available if transcription needs to be retried.
- Audio recording now uses native Android and iOS modules with a consistent 16 kHz WAV format.
- Transcription fallback handling now covers the current Gemini Flash model lineup.

### Gemini model updates

- Added Gemini 3.8 Flash High, Medium, and Low options for connected Google accounts.
- Mapped Gemini 3.8 and 3.7 Flash effort levels to the current Antigravity tiered model contract.
- Updated request handling and tests for tiered thinking levels.

### Maintenance and fixes

- Simplified tool definitions and web-search helpers without changing their public behavior.
- Removed the unfinished desktop connection path and obsolete voice-call code.
- Added a code-size policy check to catch accidental file growth during development.
- Updated provider, dictation, settings, and architecture documentation.

## How to use it

- Connect a Gemini Google account in Settings.
- Open a chat and tap the microphone in the composer to start recording.
- Tap the microphone again to transcribe the recording into your draft, then review or edit it before sending.
- Choose Gemini 3.8 Flash from the chat model picker when you want the newest Antigravity Flash option.

## Known issues

- Dictation requires a connected Gemini Google account and network access.
- Gemini Google-account models use an experimental upstream interface that may change without notice.
- Android beta installs are still manual outside Google Play.
- iOS is not part of the public beta channel yet.
